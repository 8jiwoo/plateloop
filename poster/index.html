PlateLoop

Know what’s eaten. Waste less.

An AI food scanner that measures what is served, eaten and left on every tray, so canteens cook the right amount, and everyone who eats gets their own nutrition feedback.

2Our process design thinking, from lunch queue to prototype

1

Empathise

Watched how lunch really runs, mapped four people it affects and read the research on each.

2

Define

One problem: kitchens can’t see what is eaten, dish by dish and person by person.

3

Ideate

Compared five ways to measure: bin weighing, surveys, photo apps, pre-orders and a tray scan.

4

Prototype

Modelled the scanner in 3D, built its kiosk, a kitchen dashboard, three apps and a 3D game.

5

Test

Walked every step until it broke, then turned each problem into a feature.

Face not recognised → type your class numberClean-plate pressure → compared with your own usualAlerts ignored → nurse actions in one tap“Is it filming me?” → consent, no photos leave

PlateLoop Kitchen

Canteen staff
  • Tomorrow’s order from real attendance, weather and events.
  • Waste by dish, so the kailan nobody eats gets smaller.
  • Carbon ledger: CO₂ avoided, ready for reporting.

Loopi

Students
