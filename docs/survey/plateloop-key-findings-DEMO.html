Findings and what they mean for PlateLoop

1
The most common reason for leaving food is ‘Portion too big’ (43%); 47% would take a smaller portion if they could ask easily.→ The before scan and right-portion rewards in Loopi: a small plate finished counts fully.
2
When seeing what they ate, 57% would rather see food groups and 10% calories.→ Loopi shows food groups by default; calories stay optional.
3
Interest in scanning trays to feed a virtual pet averages 4.0 out of 5; 67% agree.→ The Loopi pet as the reason students scan.
4
43% of students are comfortable with face sign-in.→ Keep the class-and-number alternative; face sign-in stays optional.
5
63% of kitchen staff decide quantities by experience or headcount; 13% know accurately which dishes come back.→ Kitchen dashboard: waste by dish and a forecast from real eating data.
6
Kitchen staff estimate 22% of cooked food is thrown away and spend 32 minutes a day planning quantities.→ A suggested order for tomorrow saves planning time and cuts over-preparation.
7
A suggested order is rated useful by 75% and waste by dish by 100%; 50% worry about extra workload.→ Measurement is automatic: no forms or manual entry for kitchen staff.
8
17% of hospital staff trust their intake records; documenting intake takes 31 minutes a shift.→ Loopi Care records every tray automatically.
9
Staff say a patient who stops eating is noticed the same day in 33% of cases.→ Nurse-station alerts after poor meals, the same day.
10
Automatic intake records are rated useful by 100%; 50% say patients would benefit from choosing tomorrow’s meals.→ Loopi Care: per-tray intake and patient meal choice.
11
33% of office workers know the nutrients in their lunch; 50% feel an afternoon energy dip often or daily.→ Loopi Work: nutrients per lunch and a 3 pm energy check-in.
12
33% would pre-order lunch to skip a typical 10-minute queue.→ Pre-ordering in Loopi Work; kitchens cook to orders.
13
17% would share meal data with their employer; 50% would share anonymous totals only.→ Private by default: employers see team totals, never names.
