Findings and what they mean for PlateLoop

1
Portion size is the top reason for leaving food: 35% chose it, and 52% would take a smaller portion if they could ask easily.→ The before scan and right-portion rewards in Loopi: a small plate finished counts fully.
2
More students prefer food groups (44%) than calories (23%).→ Loopi shows food groups by default; calories stay optional.
3
A virtual pet appeals more to secondary students (average 3.9/5) than to university students (3.5/5).→ Lead with the Loopi pet in schools; emphasise nutrition feedback on campuses.
4
Only 43% of students are comfortable with face sign-in.→ Keep the class-and-number alternative; face sign-in stays optional.
5
Most kitchen staff plan by experience or headcount (83%), and only 21% know accurately which dishes come back.→ Kitchen dashboard: waste by dish and a forecast from real eating data.
6
Staff estimate 21% of cooked food is thrown away, and spend 34 minutes a day planning quantities.→ A suggested order for tomorrow saves planning time and cuts over-preparation.
7
A suggested order is rated useful by 75% and waste by dish by 67%, but 42% worry about extra workload.→ Measurement is automatic: no forms or manual entry for kitchen staff.
8
Only 22% of hospital staff trust their intake records, and they spend 30 minutes a shift documenting intake.→ Loopi Care records every tray automatically.
9
Patients who stop eating are noticed the same day in only 14% of cases.→ Nurse-station alerts after poor meals, the same day.
10
Automatic intake records are rated useful by 83%; 61% say patients would benefit from choosing tomorrow’s meals.→ Loopi Care: per-tray intake and patient meal choice.
11
Only 15% of office workers know the nutrients in their lunch, and 43% feel an afternoon energy dip often or daily.→ Loopi Work: nutrients per lunch and a 3 pm energy check-in.
12
64% would pre-order lunch to skip a typical 10-minute queue.→ Pre-ordering in Loopi Work; kitchens cook to orders.
13
Only 13% would share meal data with their employer; 56% would share anonymous totals only.→ Private by default: employers see team totals, never names.
